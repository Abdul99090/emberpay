How it works

  1. Describe — type one sentence; the invoice assistant extracts amount, label and note (deterministic parser, runs 100% in your browser).
  2. Share — the invoice becomes a single link. No server, no account, no database: the link is the invoice.
  3. Get paid — the payer scans the QR or taps the link and confirms in their own wallet (WizardConnect or any BIP21 wallet). Settlement is on-chain BCH: seconds, ~1 sat/byte fees.

CashTokens are the engine, not an afterthought: an invoice can be denominated in any CashToken — fungible tokens or a specific NFT. The link carries the full token terms (category, amount / NFT commitment), payments land on token-aware addresses, and EmberPay's tested transaction builder assembles the token transfer with exact conservation: token change returns to the payer, nothing is silently burned. That's a payment primitive that only exists because BCH has native tokens.
